## Motivation

Research projects often produce software, ontologies, datasets and publications that are spread across different platforms. A project website should make that work understandable and citable without becoming a second content-management system.

This example project investigates how structured knowledge and lightweight web publishing can be combined so that results remain reproducible and easy to reuse.

## Objectives

1. Develop a small, reusable website theme for research projects.
2. Keep publications in a machine-readable format.
3. Make the site easy to maintain after the project ends.
